Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

1. Who we are

AdBoard is an independent Android analytics app for AdMob publishers. In this policy, “AdBoard”, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to the AdBoard app and its developer. Contact: chiranthvinod96@gmail.com.

2. Scope

This policy explains how AdBoard processes information when you install and use the app, connect an AdMob account, buy AdBoard Pro, see ads, or contact support. It does not cover Google’s own processing. Google’s Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms apply to Google Play, AdMob and Google account services.

3. Information we process

3.1 Google account and AdMob data

If you connect a real AdMob publisher account, AdBoard requests the Google OAuth scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admob.readonly. This allows AdBoard to read AdMob reporting data and account metadata. It does not allow AdBoard to change your AdMob account, campaigns, payments or settings.

The app may process Google account identifiers needed to complete OAuth and identify the connected publisher; AdMob publisher/account identifiers; app, ad-unit, country, format, ad-source and mediation metadata; estimated earnings, requests, impressions, clicks, match rate, show rate, CTR, eCPM and related metrics; report ranges and filters; alert rules and history; saved reports; and widget configuration. This data is stored primarily in a local database on your device. AdBoard is designed to be read-only and to keep your AdMob reporting data on-device.

3.2 Local app data

AdBoard stores app preferences (theme, default date range, comparison, privacy mode, biometric lock, notification preferences, widget refresh interval); the local reporting cache and saved reports; alert rules, alerts and digest preferences; widget snapshots and configurations; diagnostics counters and the last local crash log unless you clear them; and entitlement cache and ad-free session timestamps. Diagnostics stay on-device unless you explicitly use a share action.

3.3 Purchase and subscription data

If you buy AdBoard Pro, Google Play processes the payment. AdBoard does not receive your full payment card details.

To verify and manage your subscription, AdBoard and its verification backend (hosted on Google Cloud) may process the Google Play product ID and base plan (monthly/annual), purchase token and purchase state, subscription status (active, pending, grace period, account hold, paused, cancelled, expired, revoked), expiry/renewal dates, acknowledgement state, a SHA-256 hash of the purchase token used as the backend record key, an optional obfuscated account identifier used for fraud prevention, and verification results and error categories. Purchase tokens are held in encrypted storage on the device where needed. The backend stores only the hash as its record key and does not log raw purchase tokens.

3.4 Advertising data

In the Free tier, AdBoard uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The SDK may collect and share IP address; Android advertising ID, app set ID and similar device/account identifiers; ad interactions (requests, impressions, clicks, video views); app/product interactions; diagnostic and performance information; and approximate location derived from IP address where permitted. This data is used for advertising, ad measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. AdMob may serve personalised or non-personalised ads depending on your consent and settings.

Where required (for example, the EEA, UK and Switzerland), AdBoard uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to request consent and provide a privacy-options entry point. You can change your choices through that entry point. If consent is denied or unavailable, AdBoard will not request personalised ads. AdBoard does not send your AdMob earnings figures, publisher ID, ad-unit IDs, report rows, saved reports or alert content to the ads SDK as targeting data.

3.5 Analytics and diagnostics

AdBoard may record privacy-limited product events such as ad placement shown/failed/suppressed, rewarded ad started/completed/dismissed, ad-free session started/expired, Pro page viewed and upgrade prompt shown/dismissed, purchase/restore result and subscription state category, and widget/alert access blocked or allowed. These events are designed not to include AdMob earnings figures, publisher IDs, ad-unit IDs, purchase tokens, email addresses, precise location or advertising IDs. If analytics are enabled, they are used to understand product performance and improve reliability.

3.6 Support communications

If you contact support, we process the information you provide (for example, your email address, device model, app version and description of the issue). Do not send purchase tokens, passwords or full payment details.

4. Why we process information

We process information to provide the analytics, reports, drill-downs and offline cache; authenticate your Google account and read AdMob data you authorise; provide widgets, alerts and the daily digest; show and measure ads in the Free tier; provide the optional rewarded ad-free session; process, verify, restore and manage Pro subscriptions; prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorised entitlement access; maintain security, debug crashes and improve reliability; and comply with legal obligations and respond to support requests.

Where GDPR/UK GDPR applies, our legal bases are contract (providing the app and Pro service), consent (personalised advertising and optional analytics where required), legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, service reliability and limited product measurement), and legal obligation (tax, accounting, consumer-protection and platform-compliance requirements).

5. Sharing

We share information only as needed: Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention; Google Play Billing / Google Play Developer API for purchases and subscription management; our backend hosting provider for purchase verification, RTDN processing and rewarded SSV validation; professional advisers where required; and authorities where required by law or to protect rights and safety. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share AdMob reporting figures with advertisers or ad networks.

6. Retention

  • Local AdMob data, alert history, saved reports and widget state remain on your device until you disconnect/delete them, clear app data or uninstall the app.
  • OAuth tokens remain until they expire, you disconnect, or you clear app data.
  • Purchase/entitlement records are retained for up to 24 months for verification, fraud prevention, accounting and dispute resolution, unless a longer period is required by law.
  • Rewarded-ad audit records are retained for up to 90 days.
  • AdMob/advertising data is retained according to Google’s policies.
  • Support communications are retained only as long as needed to resolve the request and meet legal obligations.
  • Backend logs are retained for a short operational period and are configured not to contain raw purchase tokens or SSV signatures.

7. Security

We use HTTPS/TLS for network traffic, encrypt OAuth and purchase tokens on-device where supported, avoid logging tokens, disable Android backups for the local database, and limit backend access. No method is completely secure; protect your device with a screen lock and keep your Google account secure.

8. Home-screen widgets and privacy mode

Privacy mode masks money values inside the AdBoard app and in notification text. Home-screen widgets are intentionally exempt from privacy mode. Widget values can be visible to anyone who can see your device’s home screen. You can remove a widget at any time. Do not place AdBoard widgets on a home screen you expose to other people if you do not want them to see your AdMob figures.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information; object to or restrict certain processing; data portability; withdraw consent for personalised advertising or analytics; and lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

You can change ad consent through the in-app privacy options (where available); turn off alerts, digest or notifications in Settings; use privacy mode for in-app values; disconnect and delete local AdMob data in Settings; manage or cancel your Pro subscription in Google Play; and contact chiranthvinod96@gmail.com to exercise a rights request. See Delete your data for deletion steps.

10. Children

AdBoard is a business/analytics tool intended for AdMob publishers and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us so we can delete it.

11. International transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than yours. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers. Google’s processing is governed by its own terms and safeguards.

12. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app or legal requirements change. We will update the “Last updated” date and, where required, provide additional notice or obtain consent.
