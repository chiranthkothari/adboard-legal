Delete your data

AdBoard is a read-only AdMob analytics app. We do not create an AdBoard account. This page explains how to delete the data the app and its subscription backend hold.

Delete AdMob data and local app data

  1. Open AdBoard.
  2. Go to More → Accounts and disconnect the AdMob account. This revokes the app's Google OAuth access and deletes the local AdMob cache, saved reports, alert history and widget data.
  3. Alternatively, uninstall AdBoard or clear its app data in Android Settings. This removes all local data from the device.

Request deletion of subscription verification data

When you buy AdBoard Pro, our verification backend stores a SHA-256 hash of your Google Play purchase token, the subscription product and state, and expiry dates. It does not store your name, email, payment card details or AdMob reporting data.

To request deletion of this record, email chiranthvinod96@gmail.com with the subject “AdBoard data deletion request”. Include the Google Play account email you used to subscribe. We will delete the record within 30 days and confirm by email.

If you have an active subscription, deleting the backend record does not cancel the subscription. Cancel it in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions. Google Play keeps its own purchase and payment records under Google's policies.

What is kept

  • Aggregated, non-identifying service logs may be retained for security and reliability for up to 30 days.
  • Records that we must keep for legal, tax or fraud-prevention obligations may be retained for the period required by law.
  • Google Play, Google AdMob and Google Sign-In retain data under their own privacy policies.
