Built to be trusted with your revenue data.

Read-only

AdBoard asks only for read-only access to your AdMob data. It cannot change your account.

Official API

Data comes from the AdMob REST API, not from the console. No scraping, no passwords stored.

Stays on your device

Data lives in a local database that is excluded from Android backups. Diagnostics stay on-device unless you share them.

You are in control

Disconnect at any time and AdBoard deletes all local data. Clean logs: no tokens, no publisher IDs.

Accurate to the micro

Money is stored as exact integer micros in your account currency. Rates are calculated from raw counts, never averaged.

Questions, answered plainly.

Is AdBoard an official Google product?

No. AdBoard is an independent app for Google AdMob publishers. It is not a Google product.

Can AdBoard change my AdMob account?

No. It is a read-only analytics client. It shows data and does not change anything in your account.

Are the earnings final?

Money values are estimates until AdMob finalizes them after month end. AdBoard labels them “estimated” everywhere.

Does it work offline?

Yes. AdBoard keeps your data in a local database, so you can still see it without a connection. Screens show a “last updated” time.

Are alerts instant?

No. Alerts use complete days of data, not today’s partial numbers. Background checks run when Android allows it, because Android access tokens are short-lived.

Can I connect more than one AdMob account?

Yes. Connect several publisher accounts and switch between them.

How do I remove my data?

Disconnect in the app. AdBoard revokes access and deletes all local data.

Is there an iPhone version?

No. AdBoard is Android only — widget, biometric lock, and offline-first included.

How much does it cost?

Pricing is not final yet. The core dashboard is free; an AdBoard Pro subscription will cover full history, unlimited alerts, saved reports, export, widgets and multi-account. This page will be updated when pricing is set.
